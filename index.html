Build up the lodge

Bring cash, intel and scrap home to build. Buildings unlock cards and perks for every expedition after.

How it plays
  • Your skier skis the mountain on their own: up the lift, down the trail, back to the lodge.
  • Chairlifts climb from the trail back up the mountain: ride one to chain another descent before the lodge for +1 mult, but every descent brings tougher enemies and the lap only cashes in at the lodge. Chutes are steep shortcuts straight down.
  • Where a route branches off, a red rope closes the way you won't go. Click the junction (or press R for the next one) to switch.
  • Place cards from your hand to build it. Trail cards go on the run, roadside cards beside it, landscape cards on open snow. Enemy cards are dangerous but drop loot and more cards.
  • Steer the speed with W and S. Tuck is fast, hard to hit and builds mult, but your aim is poor. Snowplow is slow and accurate, but you're a sitting duck.
  • Fights happen on the move. Kills, grazes, air and supplies earn chips. Each lap cashes in as chips × mult at the lodge.
  • At the lodge, ride again or call it a day and keep everything. Bail out mid-lap and keep 60%; wipe out and keep 30%.
  • Every card you place fills the Baron meter. Fill it and the Avalanche Baron comes for you.
